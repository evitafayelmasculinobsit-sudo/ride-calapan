About Us

A local trip planner built for Calapan City.

RIDE started as a second-year student project with one aim: make Calapan City easier to explore.

Our background

Calapan City is the capital of Oriental Mindoro and a port city that many travelers pass through. Information about what to see and how to fit it into a day is spread across social media posts, word of mouth and scattered web pages.

RIDE brings that information into one mobile-friendly web app. Visitors and residents can discover places, plan an itinerary and save their favorites in one place.

Our mission

To help people enjoy Calapan City by making places easy to find and trips easy to plan.

What we value

Local first. We focus on Calapan City and do it well before covering anywhere else.

Simple to use. A visitor should be able to build a plan in a few taps.

Made for phones. Most people plan on the go, so RIDE is designed for small screens first.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about RIDE.

Can't find what you need? Visit the Contact Us page.

What is RIDE?

RIDE is a web app for exploring Calapan City, Oriental Mindoro. You can discover places and plan an itinerary around them.

Do I need to download anything?

No. RIDE opens in your phone or computer browser. On a phone you can also add it to your home screen for quicker access.

Which area does RIDE cover?

RIDE focuses on Calapan City in Oriental Mindoro, Philippines.

How do I keep places I like?

Use the Saved page in the app to keep your favorite places in one list. You can go back to it any time and use it to build your itinerary.

Does RIDE work on my phone?

Yes. RIDE is designed for mobile screens first and also works on laptops and desktops.

How do I report a problem or suggest a place?

Send us a message using the details on the Contact Us page. Tell us what you saw and which page you were on, and we will look into it.
